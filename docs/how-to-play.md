# How to play

Before starting to play, open the dashboard, which is located on port 80.

## Starting the game

If the game isn't started, the dashboard will either show `Game is not running` or `You died!`, depending on whether the game was played before.

To start the game, press the touch sensor on your Joy-Pi once. You will see that you can move your character now and that zombies are spawning.

## Zombies

Zombies are the obstacle in this game. They are spawning continuously and trying to attack you. To protect yourself, you can either run away from them or throw fireballs at them.

## Throwing fireballs

To throw fireballs, you need to press the touch sensor. The fireball will be thrown in the same direction as which you made your last step. If a fireball hits a zombie, it dies and your score goes up by one.
